# Known Issues

## Italics and tightly-spaced fonts

On some subtitles that have italic letters, they may not be recognized correctly and/or the spacing may be wrong. Subtitles with tightly spaced letters may also be misrecognized. This seems to be attributed partially to your distro's packaged Tesseract trained data. For example, Arch Linux's shipped English trained data is ~23 MB, and Ubuntu ships ~4 MB English trained data.

## Musical Note Recognition

Musical notation and symbols are not properly recognized by the OCR engine. Subtitles containing musical notes, staff notation, or music-related symbols are misinterpreted or omitted entirely. Consider manual correction of subtitles containing musical notation.
